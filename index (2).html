  • Cheers and Yells Competition
    Each group showcases its energy, teamwork, creativity, and school spirit through exciting cheers and yells.

    A special competition where selected candidates showcase their confidence, personality, talent, and stage presence.

Overall, the event helped us create new memories, strengthen friendships, and feel more connected as one CICI family.
